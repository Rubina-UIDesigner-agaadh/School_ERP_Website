Smart Management for Modern Preschools

AST Preschool ERP is a comprehensive cloud-based management solution designed exclusively for preschools, playgroups, nurseries, and kindergartens. It simplifies everyday administrative tasks while supporting the unique teaching and learning environment of early childhood education.

Built with the needs of preschool educators and administrators in mind, AST Preschool ERP connects school management, teachers, parents, and students through a secure and user-friendly platform. From admissions and attendance to child progress, fee management, and parent communication, every aspect of preschool management becomes simpler, faster, and more organized.

Digital Admission Management

Child Information Management

Maintain detailed child profiles, including personal information, medical details, emergency contacts, photographs, academic records, and developmental progress.

Attendance Management

Record student and staff attendance quickly while automatically notifying parents about their child's attendance status.

Activity Planning

Organize daily classroom activities, storytelling sessions, creative learning, art and craft, music, games, celebrations, and themed learning programs with ease.

Child Progress Tracking

Monitor each child's learning journey through observation-based assessments, developmental milestones, classroom participation, and progress reports.

Parent Communication

Strengthen parent engagement by sharing daily updates, attendance, homework, activity highlights, photographs, circulars, announcements, event reminders, and important notices through the parent portal and mobile application.

Fee Management

Automate fee collection, receipts, payment reminders, concessions, and financial reporting while providing parents with secure online payment options.

Staff Management

Manage teacher records, attendance, leave requests, payroll, classroom allocation, and staff information from one centralized system.

Event & Academic Calendar

Plan annual functions, sports activities, celebrations, parent meetings, field trips, classroom events, and holiday calendars with a centralized scheduling system.

Reports & Analytics

Generate detailed reports for admissions, attendance, fees, child progress, staff management, and overall preschool performance to support informed decision-making.

Secure Cloud Platform

Protect sensitive student and institutional data through role-based access, secure authentication, encrypted communication, and regular cloud backups.
