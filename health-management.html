Promoting Student Well-Being Through Smart Health Management

A healthy student is better equipped to learn, participate, and succeed. Schools have an important responsibility to maintain accurate health records, respond quickly to medical situations, and ensure the overall well-being of every student. The AST ERP Health Management Module provides a centralized platform to manage student health information, medical records, health check-ups, vaccinations, and emergency medical details.

Designed specifically for educational institutions, this module enables schools to maintain complete health histories while ensuring that authorized staff can access critical medical information whenever required.

By digitizing health management, schools can provide better care, improve communication with parents, and create a safer learning environment.

Why Schools Need Health Management

Why Schools Need Health Management

Managing health records manually often leads to incomplete information, misplaced files, and delays during medical emergencies. Schools need a secure and reliable system that allows health-related information to be updated, monitored, and accessed instantly.

AST ERP Health Management helps institutions organize health records, monitor student wellness, and support informed decision-making regarding student care.

Complete Health Management

Complete Health Management

Student Health Profiles

Maintain comprehensive health profiles that include blood group, allergies, medical conditions, chronic illnesses, disabilities, emergency contacts, medications, and healthcare providers.

Medical History

Store complete medical records including previous illnesses, surgeries, treatments, hospitalization details, and doctor's recommendations.

Vaccination Records

Maintain vaccination schedules and immunization history to ensure every student's records remain complete and up to date.

Health Check-ups

Record periodic health screenings such as height, weight, vision, hearing, dental examinations, BMI, and general medical assessments.

Medication Management

Maintain records of prescribed medications and dosage instructions for students who require medical support during school hours.

Medical Incident Reporting

Record accidents, injuries, first-aid treatments, emergency situations, and actions taken by school authorities.

Parent Communication

Notify parents about medical incidents, health concerns, scheduled health camps, vaccination reminders, and wellness programs through the parent portal and mobile application.
