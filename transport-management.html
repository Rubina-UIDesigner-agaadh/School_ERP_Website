Transport Management

Safe, Efficient & Intelligent School Transportation Management

School transportation plays a vital role in ensuring students travel safely and reach school on time. Managing vehicles, routes, drivers, schedules, transport fees, and student allocations manually can be complex and time-consuming.

The AST ERP Transport Management Module provides a centralized platform to streamline every aspect of school transportation. From route planning and vehicle management to student allocation and transport fee integration, the module helps schools deliver a safe, organized, and efficient transportation service.

Designed for educational institutions of all sizes, AST ERP simplifies transport administration while improving communication between schools and parents.

Why Schools Need Transport Management

Why Schools Need Transport Management

Traditional transport management often relies on paper records, spreadsheets, and manual communication, making it difficult to monitor routes, vehicles, and student transportation efficiently.

AST ERP digitizes transport operations by providing real-time visibility into transport records, reducing administrative effort, and improving overall transport management.

Complete Transport Management

Complete Transport Management

Vehicle Management

Maintain detailed information about every school vehicle, including registration number, seating capacity, insurance details, permit validity, maintenance schedules, and assigned routes.

Route Management

Create and manage transport routes based on geographical locations, pickup points, and student distribution to optimize travel efficiency.

Stop Management

Configure pickup and drop-off locations with route mapping, estimated timings, and student assignments.

Student Transport Allocation

Assign students to vehicles and routes based on their selected pickup points while maintaining accurate transport records.

Driver & Staff Management

Maintain records of drivers, conductors, and transport staff, including contact information, license details, duty assignments, and service history.

Transport Fee Integration

Automatically link transportation services with the Fee Management Module, allowing transport charges to be included in student fee structures.

Transport Reports

Generate detailed reports on vehicle usage, route allocation, transport fees, driver assignments, student transport lists, and route-wise transportation statistics.
