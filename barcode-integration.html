Barcode Integration

Faster, Smarter & More Accurate Operations with Barcode Integration

Educational institutions manage thousands of physical resources every day, including books, inventory items, laboratory equipment, student identity cards, certificates, and institutional assets. Manual identification and tracking can be slow and prone to errors.

The AST ERP Barcode Integration Module enables schools to identify, track, and manage resources quickly using barcode technology. By automating scanning and record updates, schools can improve operational efficiency while reducing manual data entry.
