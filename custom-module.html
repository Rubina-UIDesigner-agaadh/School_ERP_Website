Custom Module in ERP

Need a unique ERP module? AST can create it.

Every educational institution has its own policies, approval flows, departments, reports, and operating style. AST School ERP can be extended with custom modules designed around your specific requirements.

This option is useful when your institution needs a workflow that is not covered by standard ERP modules, or when you want a dedicated module for a special process, dashboard, form, report, approval flow, or internal operation.
