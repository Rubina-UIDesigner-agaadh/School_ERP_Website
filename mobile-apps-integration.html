Stay Connected Anytime, Anywhere with AST ERP Mobile Applications

Education no longer happens only inside classrooms or administrative offices. Schools need the flexibility to access information, communicate instantly, and perform important tasks from anywhere. The AST ERP Mobile Applications bring the power of your school management system directly to smartphones and tablets, ensuring that administrators, teachers, parents, students, and employees remain connected at all times.

Designed with a modern, user-friendly interface, the AST ERP mobile apps provide secure access to essential features while delivering a seamless experience across Android and iOS devices.

Why Schools Need Mobile Applications

Why Schools Need Mobile Applications

Today's school community expects instant access to information. Parents want real-time updates about their children, teachers need quick access to classroom information, and administrators require operational insights even when they are away from the campus.

AST ERP Mobile Apps provide convenient access to school information while reducing paperwork, improving communication, and supporting faster decision-making.

Mobile Apps for Every User

Mobile Apps for Every User

Parent App

Parents can monitor their child's attendance, academic performance, examination results, homework, fee payments, transport information, school notices, events, and communication from a single application.

Student App

Students can access class schedules, assignments, examination timetables, results, learning resources, announcements, and important academic updates anytime.

Teacher App

Teachers can record attendance, manage assignments, enter assessment marks, communicate with parents, view class schedules, and access academic information directly from their mobile devices.

Employee App

Employees can view attendance records, apply for leave, access salary slips, receive HR notifications, and manage personal information securely.

Administrator App

School administrators can monitor admissions, attendance, fee collections, staff information, operational reports, announcements, and institutional performance through executive dashboards.

Real-Time Notifications

Receive instant push notifications for attendance, fee reminders, announcements, examination schedules, homework, transport updates, events, and emergency communications.
