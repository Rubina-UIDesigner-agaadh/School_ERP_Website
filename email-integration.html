Professional Email Communication for Modern Educational Institutions

Email remains one of the most effective communication channels for formal school correspondence. The AST ERP Email Integration Module enables institutions to send professional emails directly from the ERP platform, ensuring timely and organized communication with parents, students, teachers, staff, and external stakeholders.

From admission confirmations and fee receipts to examination reports and institutional announcements, every email is delivered efficiently while maintaining a complete communication history.
