Inventory Management

Efficient Inventory Control for Smarter School Operations

Educational institutions use a wide variety of materials every day, including stationery, laboratory equipment, classroom supplies, sports items, uniforms, teaching resources, and office consumables. Managing these resources manually often leads to stock shortages, over-purchasing, misplaced items, and unnecessary expenses.

The AST ERP Inventory Management Module provides a centralized system for tracking inventory, monitoring stock levels, managing purchases, and maintaining complete visibility over institutional resources.

By digitizing inventory operations, schools can improve accountability, optimize resource utilization, and reduce operational costs.

Why Schools Need Inventory Management

Why Schools Need Inventory Management

Without a structured inventory system, schools often struggle to maintain accurate stock records and monitor inventory movement.

AST ERP enables administrators to monitor inventory in real time, ensuring essential materials are always available while reducing wastage and improving purchasing decisions.
