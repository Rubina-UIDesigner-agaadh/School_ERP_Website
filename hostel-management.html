Hostel Management

Smart Hostel Management for Safe, Organized & Comfortable Student Living

Managing a school hostel involves much more than assigning rooms. Educational institutions must efficiently manage student accommodation, room allocation, attendance, hostel fees, visitor records, maintenance, and communication while ensuring the safety and well-being of every resident.

The AST ERP Hostel Management Module provides a centralized digital platform that simplifies hostel administration and enables institutions to efficiently manage daily hostel operations. From room allocation and occupancy tracking to fee collection and visitor management, every aspect of hostel administration is organized through one integrated system.

Designed for schools with residential facilities, the module improves operational efficiency while creating a safe, transparent, and well-managed hostel environment.

Why Schools Need Hostel Management

Why Schools Need Hostel Management

Manual hostel management often involves multiple registers, paper records, and disconnected processes, making it difficult to track room occupancy, monitor residents, and manage hostel administration efficiently.

AST ERP digitizes hostel operations by centralizing student accommodation records, automating administrative tasks, and providing real-time information to authorized staff.

Hostel Information Management

Maintain complete information about hostel buildings, floors, wings, room categories, room capacities, facilities, and occupancy status.

Room Allocation

Allocate rooms based on class, gender, capacity, availability, or institutional policies while preventing duplicate allocations and overbooking.

Student Hostel Records

Maintain complete hostel profiles including admission details, room allocation history, emergency contacts, guardian information, medical details, and hostel stay records.

Hostel Attendance

Record daily hostel attendance to monitor student presence and ensure accountability.

Visitor Management

Maintain visitor records, entry and exit timings, visitor purpose, relationship details, and approvals for every hostel visit.

Hostel Fee Management

Integrate hostel charges with the Fee Management Module to automate fee generation, payment tracking, and receipt generation.

Maintenance Management

Track room maintenance requests, repair work, housekeeping schedules, and facility maintenance activities to ensure comfortable student living.

Hostel Reports

Generate reports including room occupancy, hostel residents, fee collection, vacant rooms, visitor records, attendance, and maintenance history.
