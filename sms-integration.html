Instant School Communication Through Smart SMS Integration

Effective communication is essential for every educational institution. Schools need a reliable way to instantly share important information with parents, students, teachers, and staff. The AST ERP SMS Integration Module enables schools to send automated and personalized SMS notifications directly from the ERP system, ensuring that important messages are delivered quickly and efficiently.

Whether it's attendance alerts, fee reminders, examination schedules, admission updates, holiday announcements, or emergency notifications, SMS Integration helps schools maintain seamless communication without manual effort.

Why Schools Need SMS Integration

Why Schools Need SMS Integration

Parents may not always have internet access or actively check mobile applications. SMS remains one of the fastest and most reliable communication channels, ensuring important information reaches recipients instantly.

AST ERP automates SMS delivery based on real-time events, eliminating manual messaging while improving communication efficiency.
