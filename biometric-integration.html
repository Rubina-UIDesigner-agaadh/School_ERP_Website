Accurate Attendance Through Advanced Biometric Integration

Recording attendance accurately is essential for both students and employees. Manual attendance registers and traditional methods are time-consuming and may be vulnerable to errors or proxy attendance.

The AST ERP Biometric Integration Module connects with biometric attendance devices to automatically capture attendance data and synchronize it with the ERP system in real time.

By integrating biometric technology with attendance, HR, payroll, and reporting modules, schools can improve accuracy, strengthen accountability, and automate attendance management.
