Student Management

A Complete Digital Record for Every Student

Students are the heart of every educational institution, and managing their information accurately is essential for smooth academic and administrative operations. The AST ERP Student Management Module provides a centralized digital repository that stores and manages every student's information throughout their educational journey.

From admission to graduation, every record—including personal details, academic history, attendance, examination results, health information, transportation, fee records, and documents—is maintained securely within a single integrated system.

By eliminating manual registers and scattered records, AST ERP enables schools to access accurate student information instantly while improving efficiency, transparency, and decision-making.

Why Student Management Matters

Why Student Management Matters

Maintaining student information manually often leads to duplicate records, misplaced documents, inconsistent data, and time-consuming administrative work.

AST ERP transforms student management into a structured digital process where every department works with the same accurate and up-to-date information.

This ensures better coordination across academics, administration, finance, examinations, transport, communication, and reporting.
