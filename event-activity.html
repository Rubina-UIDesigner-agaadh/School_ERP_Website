School life extends beyond classrooms. Annual functions, sports days, cultural programs, competitions, educational tours, workshops, celebrations, and extracurricular activities play an important role in developing students' personalities and skills.

The AST ERP Event & Activity Management Module helps schools efficiently plan, organize, coordinate, and manage every institutional event from start to finish while keeping students, parents, teachers, and administrators informed through real-time communication.

Why Schools Need Event Management
