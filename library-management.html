Library Management

Modern Library Management for Smarter Learning

A well-managed library plays an essential role in supporting academic excellence by providing students and educators with easy access to knowledge and learning resources. Managing library operations manually can be time-consuming, particularly when handling large collections of books, journals, digital resources, and member records.

The AST ERP Library Management Module simplifies library administration by automating catalog management, book circulation, member records, issue and return processes, fines, and reporting through one integrated platform.

Designed specifically for educational institutions, the module helps librarians manage resources efficiently while enhancing the overall library experience for students and staff.

Why Schools Need Digital Library Management

Why Schools Need Digital Library Management

Traditional library systems often rely on paper registers and manual tracking, making it difficult to locate books, monitor availability, and maintain accurate circulation records.

AST ERP digitizes library operations, enabling schools to organize their collections, improve accessibility, and provide faster library services.
