Smart Attendance Tracking for Students and Staff

Attendance is one of the most important indicators of academic engagement and institutional discipline. The AST ERP Attendance Management Module enables schools to record, monitor, and analyze attendance accurately while eliminating time-consuming manual registers.

Whether tracking student attendance, teacher attendance, or staff attendance, the module provides real-time visibility, automated notifications, and detailed reports that help schools improve accountability and operational efficiency.
