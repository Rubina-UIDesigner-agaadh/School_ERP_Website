Front Office Management

Simplify School Administration with Smart Front Office Management

The front office is the first point of interaction between a school and its students, parents, visitors, vendors, and prospective admissions. Efficient front office operations create a positive impression while ensuring smooth administrative coordination.

The AST ERP Front Office Management Module digitizes daily front office activities, helping schools manage enquiries, visitor records, appointments, phone calls, courier management, complaints, and document tracking from a single centralized platform.

Designed to improve efficiency and transparency, the module reduces paperwork while ensuring every interaction is properly recorded and easily accessible.

Why Front Office Management Matters

Why Front Office Management Matters

Traditional front office operations often rely on paper registers, manual visitor logs, handwritten appointment books, and scattered records. This makes information difficult to retrieve and increases the risk of errors.

AST ERP transforms the front office into a digital service center where every enquiry, visitor, appointment, and communication is recorded securely and managed efficiently.
