Fees Management

Intelligent Fee Collection Made Simple

Managing school fees is one of the most critical administrative responsibilities for any educational institution. The AST ERP Fees Management Module simplifies the entire fee collection process by automating fee planning, invoicing, online payments, concessions, scholarships, reminders, receipts, and financial reporting.

Designed to reduce manual work and improve financial transparency, the module enables schools to manage fee operations efficiently while providing parents with a convenient and secure payment experience.

Smarter Financial Management

Smarter Financial Management

Traditional fee collection often involves manual calculations, paper receipts, long queues, and delayed payment tracking.

AST ERP digitizes the complete fee management process, ensuring accurate calculations, timely collections, instant receipts, and real-time financial visibility for administrators.
