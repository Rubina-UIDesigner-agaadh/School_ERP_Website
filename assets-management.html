Asset Management

Intelligent Asset Management for Better Resource Utilization

Educational institutions invest significantly in physical assets such as computers, projectors, laboratory equipment, classroom furniture, smart boards, office equipment, vehicles, sports equipment, and other infrastructure. Managing these valuable assets manually often leads to poor tracking, inefficient utilization, unexpected maintenance costs, and asset loss.

The AST ERP Asset Management Module provides a centralized system to track, monitor, maintain, and manage institutional assets throughout their lifecycle. From asset procurement and allocation to maintenance, depreciation, transfers, and disposal, every stage is managed efficiently through one integrated platform.

By digitizing asset management, schools gain complete visibility into institutional resources while improving accountability and operational efficiency.

Why Schools Need Asset Management

Why Schools Need Asset Management

As educational institutions grow, managing hundreds or even thousands of assets becomes increasingly challenging. Manual asset registers make it difficult to monitor ownership, maintenance schedules, warranty periods, and asset utilization.

AST ERP enables schools to maintain accurate asset records, reduce losses, improve maintenance planning, and maximize the value of every institutional investment.

Complete Asset Lifecycle Management

Complete Asset Lifecycle Management

Asset Registration

Register every institutional asset with complete information, including asset name, category, serial number, purchase details, supplier information, warranty period, location, and current status.

Asset Categorization

Organize assets by departments, buildings, classrooms, laboratories, libraries, offices, sports facilities, or any custom category defined by the institution.

Asset Allocation

Assign assets to departments, classrooms, laboratories, employees, or specific locations while maintaining complete allocation history and accountability.

Asset Transfers

Transfer assets between departments, campuses, or locations while maintaining a complete movement history.

Maintenance Management

Schedule preventive maintenance, record repair activities, monitor service history, and manage maintenance expenses to extend the lifespan of institutional assets.

Warranty & AMC Tracking

Track warranty periods, Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC), renewal dates, and vendor support information to ensure timely maintenance and service.

Asset Depreciation

Maintain depreciation records and asset valuation based on institutional accounting policies to support financial planning and auditing.

Asset Disposal

Manage the retirement, disposal, sale, or replacement of obsolete assets while preserving complete historical records.
