About AST

Technology Built to Make School Management Simpler

AST (Agaadh SoftTech) creates software solutions that help schools, colleges, and educational institutions move from paperwork and scattered spreadsheets to a connected digital system.

Our school ERP platform is designed to simplify everyday operations such as admissions, student records, attendance, fees, exams, report cards, parent communication, transport, staff management, and institutional reporting.

We believe technology should be easy to adopt, simple to use, and powerful enough to support long-term growth.
