Student Assessments

Measuring Student Growth with Comprehensive Assessment Management

Assessments play a vital role in understanding a student's academic progress, strengths, and areas for improvement. The AST ERP Student Assessments Module provides schools with a complete digital platform to plan, conduct, evaluate, and analyze assessments throughout the academic year.

From periodic tests and unit assessments to term examinations, practical evaluations, assignments, projects, and activity-based assessments, the module enables educators to manage every stage of the assessment process with accuracy and efficiency.

Why Digital Assessments Matter

Why Digital Assessments Matter

Traditional assessment management often involves manual mark entry, lengthy calculations, paper-based report preparation, and delayed result publication.

AST ERP automates these processes, reducing administrative effort while ensuring faster result generation and greater accuracy.
