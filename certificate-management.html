Certificate Management

Fast, Accurate & Secure Certificate Generation

Educational institutions issue various certificates throughout a student's academic journey. Preparing these documents manually often consumes valuable administrative time and increases the risk of errors.

The AST ERP Certificate Management Module enables schools to design, generate, manage, and issue professional certificates digitally with speed, consistency, and accuracy.

From Transfer Certificates and Bonafide Certificates to Character Certificates, Study Certificates, Migration Certificates, Fee Certificates, and Achievement Certificates, every document can be created using predefined templates with automatic data population.
