About AST School ERP

Empowering Schools Through Smart Digital Management

AST School ERP is a comprehensive cloud-based School Management System designed to simplify, automate, and enhance every aspect of school administration. Built with the needs of modern educational institutions in mind, AST ERP connects administrators, teachers, students, parents, and management on a single intelligent platform.

From admissions and attendance to academics, examinations, finance, transport, communication, and analytics, AST ERP streamlines daily operations while reducing paperwork and manual effort. Our solution enables schools to make informed decisions through real-time data, improve collaboration among stakeholders, and deliver a better educational experience.

Whether you manage a preschool, K-12 school, college, or a multi-branch institution, AST ERP adapts to your workflows and grows with your organization.
