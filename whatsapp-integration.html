Connect with Parents Through the World's Most Popular Messaging Platform

WhatsApp has become one of the most widely used communication platforms, making it an ideal channel for schools to stay connected with parents, students, teachers, and staff. The AST ERP WhatsApp Integration Module enables institutions to send automated notifications, reminders, documents, and announcements directly through WhatsApp.

By integrating WhatsApp with AST ERP, schools can improve communication speed, increase message visibility, and enhance parent engagement through a familiar and convenient platform.

Why Schools Need WhatsApp Integration

Why Schools Need WhatsApp Integration

Parents are more likely to view WhatsApp messages immediately compared to emails or printed notices. Using WhatsApp allows schools to communicate faster while reducing dependence on paper-based communication.

AST ERP automates WhatsApp messaging based on real-time events, ensuring that important information reaches recipients without delay.
