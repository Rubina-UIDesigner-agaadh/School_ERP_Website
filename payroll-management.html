Accurate, Automated & Efficient Payroll Processing

Payroll is one of the most critical administrative functions in any educational institution. Managing employee salaries, allowances, deductions, taxes, reimbursements, and statutory compliance manually can be time-consuming and prone to calculation errors.

The AST ERP Payroll Management Module automates the complete payroll process, ensuring employees are paid accurately and on time while significantly reducing administrative effort.

Designed specifically for schools, the module integrates seamlessly with HR, attendance, and leave management to provide a complete payroll solution.

Why Schools Need Payroll Automation
