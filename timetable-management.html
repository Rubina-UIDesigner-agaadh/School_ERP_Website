An efficient timetable is the backbone of a well-managed educational institution. It ensures that classes, teachers, subjects, and classrooms are organized effectively, enabling smooth academic operations while maximizing the utilization of available resources.

The AST ERP Timetable Management Module simplifies the complex process of timetable creation by providing a centralized platform where administrators can plan, organize, and manage class schedules with accuracy and flexibility.

Whether your institution manages a single campus or multiple branches, AST ERP helps create conflict-free timetables while reducing manual planning and scheduling errors.
