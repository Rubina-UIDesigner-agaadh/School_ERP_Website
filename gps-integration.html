Real-Time School Transportation Monitoring with GPS Integration

Student safety is one of the highest priorities for every educational institution. Parents expect visibility into their child's school transportation, while administrators require accurate monitoring of school vehicles and transport operations.

The AST ERP GPS Integration Module connects school vehicles with GPS tracking systems, enabling real-time vehicle monitoring, route tracking, estimated arrival times, and transport notifications.

This integration enhances transportation safety while improving operational efficiency and communication between schools and parents.

Why Schools Need GPS Integration

Why Schools Need GPS Integration

Traditional transport systems provide limited visibility into vehicle movement, making it difficult to respond quickly to delays or route changes.

AST ERP GPS Integration provides live vehicle tracking, helping schools manage transportation more effectively while giving parents greater peace of mind.
