Employee Leave Management

Simplify Leave Administration with Intelligent Leave Tracking

Managing employee leave efficiently is essential for maintaining uninterrupted school operations and ensuring fair workforce management. The AST ERP Employee Leave Management Module automates the complete leave process, allowing employees to submit leave requests digitally while enabling administrators to review, approve, and monitor leave records with ease.

The module minimizes paperwork, improves transparency, and helps schools maintain accurate leave balances, attendance records, and workforce availability throughout the academic year.

Why Digital Leave Management Matters

Why Digital Leave Management Matters

Traditional leave management often involves manual applications, paper approvals, and complicated leave calculations that consume valuable administrative time.

AST ERP streamlines the process by creating a transparent digital workflow where every leave request is tracked from submission to approval.

Complete Leave Management

Complete Leave Management

Leave Policy Configuration

Create institution-specific leave policies for different employee categories, including casual leave, sick leave, earned leave, maternity leave, paternity leave, and special leave.

Online Leave Application

Employees can submit leave requests through the employee portal or mobile application by selecting leave type, duration, and reason.

Approval Workflow

Administrators and reporting managers receive leave requests instantly and can approve, reject, or request additional information through a structured approval process.

Leave Balance Tracking

Automatically calculate available leave balances, used leave, pending leave requests, and carry-forward leave according to institutional policies.

Leave Calendar

View employee leave schedules through a centralized calendar to ensure proper workforce planning and avoid staff shortages.

Attendance Integration

Approved leave requests are automatically reflected in employee attendance records and payroll calculations, eliminating duplicate entries.
