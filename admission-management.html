Admission Management

Simplifying the Student Admission Journey from Enquiry to Enrollment

Admissions are the first interaction between a school and a prospective student. A smooth, transparent, and well-organized admission process creates a positive first impression for parents while reducing the administrative workload for school staff.

The AST ERP Admission Management Module is designed to digitize and automate every stage of the admission lifecycle. From handling initial enquiries and online registrations to document verification, merit processing, fee collection, and final enrollment, the module ensures every admission is managed efficiently and accurately.

Whether your institution admits a few hundred students or thousands every academic year, AST ERP provides a centralized platform that streamlines the entire process while maintaining complete transparency and accountability.

Why Schools Need Digital Admission Management

Why Schools Need Digital Admission Management

Traditional admission processes often involve manual forms, multiple registers, paperwork, repeated data entry, and long waiting periods. These challenges increase the possibility of errors, misplaced documents, and communication gaps with parents.

AST ERP transforms this experience by creating a fully digital admission workflow that allows administrators to monitor every application in real time while providing parents with a simple and convenient admission experience.

Complete Admission Lifecycle Management

Complete Admission Lifecycle Management

The Admission Management Module supports every stage of student enrollment through a structured and organized workflow.

01

Admission Enquiry Management

Capture and manage enquiries received through walk-ins, phone calls, emails, websites, or admission campaigns.

Maintain complete enquiry records, schedule follow-ups, and monitor conversion rates to improve admissions.

02

Online Registration

Allow parents to complete admission forms online from anywhere.

The system securely collects student information, parent details, academic history, and required documents while reducing paperwork and manual data entry.

03

Application Verification

School administrators can verify submitted applications, review uploaded documents, validate information, and communicate with parents whenever additional information is required.

This ensures that every application meets institutional requirements before moving to the next stage.

04

Admission Screening

Schools can conduct entrance examinations, interviews, counselling sessions, or merit evaluations depending on their admission policy.

All assessment results can be recorded digitally, making candidate evaluation transparent and organized.

05

Admission Approval

Once verification is complete, administrators can approve applications with a single action.

Approved applicants automatically move to the enrollment stage without requiring duplicate data entry.

06

Fee Collection

Generate admission fees, registration charges, security deposits, and other applicable payments directly through the system.

Parents can receive digital receipts and complete payments using integrated online payment gateways.

07

Student Enrollment

Upon successful admission, the applicant is automatically converted into an active student profile within AST ERP.

The student's information becomes immediately available across attendance, academics, examinations, transport, library, communication, and other modules without entering data again.
