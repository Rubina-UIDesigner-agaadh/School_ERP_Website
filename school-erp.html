AST School ERP

Complete Digital Management for Modern Schools

AST School ERP is a comprehensive cloud-based school management platform designed to simplify every aspect of school administration. It connects school management, administrators, teachers, parents, students, and support staff through one secure and intelligent system.

From admissions and academics to examinations, finance, HR, transport, library, communication, and analytics, AST School ERP helps educational institutions automate routine tasks, improve operational efficiency, and deliver an exceptional learning experience.

Whether you manage a single campus or multiple branches, AST School ERP provides the flexibility and scalability required to meet the growing needs of modern education.
