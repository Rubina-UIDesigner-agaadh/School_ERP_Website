Online Payment Integration

Secure, Fast & Convenient Digital Fee Collection

Digital payments have become an essential part of modern education. Parents expect the convenience of paying school fees online without visiting the school or waiting in long queues. The AST ERP Online Payment Integration Module enables educational institutions to collect payments securely through multiple digital payment methods while simplifying financial operations.

Integrated with the AST ERP Fee Management Module, the system provides real-time payment updates, automatic receipt generation, and transparent transaction tracking for both schools and parents.

Why Schools Need Online Payment Integration

Why Schools Need Online Payment Integration

Manual fee collection consumes significant administrative time and often involves cash handling, cheque processing, reconciliation, and delayed payment updates.

AST ERP eliminates these challenges by providing a secure online payment platform that automates payment processing and financial reconciliation.
